01 / Our purpose

The future of ocean farming starts in the genome.

Oyster Genomics builds the genetic operating infrastructure for commercial aquaculture, so ocean farming can become predictable, programmable bio-manufacturing.

The challenge / 01

Wild genetics.
Commercial expectations.

Aquaculture produces more than half the world’s seafood. The genetics underneath it have not kept pace with the demands of commercial farming.

The challenge / 02

Disease changes
the economics.

Endemic pathogens can devastate a crop. High mortality forces farmers to over-seed, compressing margins and limiting access to capital.

The opportunity / 03

Build resilience
at the source.

Resolve the regulatory nodes that govern immunity. Carry those changes into parent lines, then into the seed a farmer plants.

02 / The platformSwitchpoint™

Read the circuitry.
Rewrite the outcome.

Switchpoint reads the bivalve genome as dynamic, rewritable circuitry. It locates the structural variants behind innate immunity and growth, then translates them into improved parent lines.

Chromosome-scale assemblies and long-read structural-variant maps locate the regulatory nodes that control innate immunity and growth.
