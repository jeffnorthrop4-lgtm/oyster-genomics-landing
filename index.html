01 / Why this market is stuck

Half the seafood is farmed.
The crop is still a bet.

Farms produce about half the world’s aquatic animals.1 Yet oyster farming still faces losses that can take most of a planting. Uncertain harvests make margins, insurance, and lending harder.

On land, seed companies hold the parent lines and farmers buy the seed each season. Oyster Genomics is building that business for ocean farms.

The crop / 01

The crop fails.

Disease and marine heat can devastate a planting. Farmers over-seed to cover losses, tying up money before a harvest is certain.

The cycle / 02

Breeding takes years.

Selecting survivors is only part of the job. Large rearranged stretches of DNA can be hard to track and carry into parent stock through a multi-year breeding cycle.

The business / 03

The parents matter.

A better oyster has to reach the hatchery parents that supply farms. A repeatable seed business needs a repeatable parent line.

03 / Why oysters, and why now

The customer
already exists.

Commercial oyster farms on the US East Coast already buy hatchery seed. Triploid seed is an established format in Virginia.2 We do not have to invent the channel. We have to put a better line into parents hatcheries already know how to cross.

Long-read sequencing can now resolve large DNA rearrangements that short reads struggle to see.3 That is the timing: more readable genetics, and a hatchery system that can multiply a parent.

05 / How we get paid

The seed business.
Every season.

Hatcheries pay for access to the parent lines. A royalty follows every thousand units of seed sold to farms. The next planting starts with another purchase.

Eastern oyster seed on the US East Coast and Gulf is the market we claim. Other farmed shellfish may fit the same parent-and-seed model later.

Access fee
Regional rights to use the parent lines at a licensed hatchery.
Seed royalty
A payment on every thousand units of seed sold to farms.
Parents stay. Seed ships.
The parent lines stay with the licensed hatchery. Farms buy seed for each planting and never receive a parent.

Triploid seed is the commercial format, not a perfect biological seal. The protection is custody of the parents, backed by the license contract. Farms receive a crop to grow, not their own breeding program.

06 / The path to market

Review before sale.

Lines carrying an intentional DNA change will be sold only through an FDA-reviewed pathway. FDA uses a risk-based approach. “Non-transgenic” is not an exemption, and this site does not claim one.4
